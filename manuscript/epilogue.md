# Epilogue: Now Go

You have the mission. You have the victory conditions. You have the honest reckoning with everything the institution got wrong and the clarity to carry the mission without repeating it.

What you don't have is a community.

The early church in Acts was not a building, a denomination, or a brand. It was people. They shared meals. They pooled resources. They argued about what the mission required and then did the work. They had no cathedral, no hierarchy, no creed longer than two commandments. They had each other.

That is what we are building. Not a new church — the original one. A unified, global body of people committed to the mission: love God, love your neighbor, and carry that love until the victory conditions are met. No borders between members. No denominations. No gatekeepers deciding who belongs. If you are willing to carry the mission, you belong.

This is an invitation.

**Join the community.** We are gathering at [thechurch.one](https://www.thechurch.one) — a place to connect with others who have read this story and decided to carry it. Come as you are. Bring your doubt. Bring your questions. Bring your willingness to do the hard work.

**Support the mission.** If you believe humanity is worth saving — if the victory conditions are worth pursuing across generations — then put something behind that belief. We are raising funds to build God's kingdom on Earth, openly and transparently: [gofundme.com/f/open-source-society](https://www.gofundme.com/f/open-source-society)

You just spent twenty-four hours with the full story of what God asked humanity to do and why it matters. The question is no longer whether you understand the mission. The question is what you will do about it.

Choose life. Start today.
